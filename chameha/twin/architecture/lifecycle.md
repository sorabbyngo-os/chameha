# Twin Lifecycle

Every Twin progresses through a lifecycle.

---

## Stage 1

Creation

Identity is established.

No observations exist.

---

## Stage 2

Observation

Signals begin arriving.

The Twin starts learning.

---

## Stage 3

Memory

Experiences accumulate.

The Twin begins remembering.

---

## Stage 4

Knowledge

Patterns emerge.

Facts are extracted.

Relationships are built.

---

## Stage 5

Model Formation

The Twin becomes a living representation.

---

## Stage 6

Prediction

Future states become possible.

---

## Stage 7

Simulation

Alternative futures are explored.

---

## Stage 8

Decision Support

Recommendations become available.

---

## Stage 9

Autonomous Assistance

The Twin may execute delegated tasks.

---

## Stage 10

Continuous Evolution

The Twin continuously improves.

There is no terminal state.

The Twin evolves for its entire lifetime.
