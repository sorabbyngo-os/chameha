# Decision Engine

> The Decision Engine evaluates alternatives and recommends the best course of action.

---

# Purpose

Planning generates possibilities.

Decision selects among them.

The Decision Engine weighs objectives, risks, constraints and confidence before recommending an action.

---

# Vision

Make rational, explainable and trustworthy decisions.

---

# Responsibilities

The Decision Engine

- Compare plans
- Evaluate trade-offs
- Assess risks
- Balance objectives
- Resolve conflicts
- Produce recommendations
- Explain reasoning

---

# Decision Factors

The engine evaluates

- Goals
- Constraints
- Risk
- Cost
- Time
- Ethics
- Permissions
- Confidence
- Historical outcomes

# Explainability

Every recommendation should answer

Why?

Why not?

What assumptions were made?

What risks exist?

What alternatives were rejected?

---

# Principles

Recommendations are explainable.

High-risk decisions require explicit approval.

Permissions always override intelligence.
