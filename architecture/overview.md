# Chameha Architecture

Chameha is the user-facing intelligence of Sorabbyngo OS.

It is responsible for:

- Voice interaction
- Text interaction
- Intent understanding
- Conversation management
- Task execution
- Personalization
- Device control
- Automation
- AION orchestration

Chameha does not perform distributed reasoning itself.

Complex reasoning is delegated to Byngox.

Networking is delegated to Byngonet.

The result is returned to the user through a natural conversational interface.
